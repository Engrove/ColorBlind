Color Name Camera

Point the camera at a wire, hose, panel, paint surface, component, or material. The app samples the center target and shows both the closest exact color name and the broad color family.

Camera color depends on light, white balance, shadows, glare, dirt, and material finish. Do not use this as the only decision source for safety-critical work.
